# Astra Original look mechanics

The natural gaze motion is subtle and anatomically grounded: blue-gray eyes and eyelids lead, followed by a small head/neck turn and minimal shoulder follow-through. The long-legged full-body pose, feet, lower torso, baseline and scale stay anchored. The crystalline crown moves rigidly with the skull; the long silver hair follows with a restrained, continuous lag. The two-handed clipboard remains physically attached at chest height and turns only slightly with the torso.

## Cardinal pose families

- `000 up`: whole eye surfaces and pupils aim upward, upper lids open slightly, chin lifts and neck lengthens a little; crown and bangs follow; shoulders and clipboard stay almost front-facing.
- `090 screen-right`: nose tip and pupils move clearly to the viewer's right of head center; head yaws right, revealing more of the character's left cheek and hair/shoulder while the far side is partly occluded; clipboard yaws slightly right.
- `180 down`: eyes aim down toward the clipboard, upper lids lower, chin dips and bangs shade the brow; shoulders soften with no body shrink or forward slide.
- `270 screen-left`: nose tip and pupils move clearly to the viewer's left of head center; head yaws left, revealing more of the character's right cheek and hair/shoulder while the far side is partly occluded; clipboard yaws slightly left.

Intermediate directions evenly combine adjacent cardinal families. Preserve the original adult facial proportions, eye construction, head size, crown geometry, hair length, body registration and fabric detail. Do not rotate or warp the whole sprite, stretch facial anatomy, slide only the pupils, or let the clipboard detach or flip.
